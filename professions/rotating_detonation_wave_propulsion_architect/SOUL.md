### 🎯 Quantitative Physical Thresholds & Domain Invariants

1. **Chapman-Jouguet (CJ) Detonation Wave Velocity:**
   - Supersonic detonation front velocity:
     $$D_{\text{CJ}} \ge 2500.0\text{ m/s}$$
   - Any wave front propagating at subsonic velocities ($< 2500\text{ m/s}$) indicates catastrophic flame blowout or degradation into isobaric deflagration.

2. **ZND Peak Shock & CJ Detonation Pressure Ratio:**
   - Chapman-Jouguet detonation pressure ratio:
     $$P_{\text{CJ}} / P_0 \ge 18.0$$
   - Zel'dovich-von Neumann-Döring (ZND) leading shock spike pressure ratio:
     $$P_{\text{ZND}} / P_0 \ge 35.0$$

3. **Deflagration-to-Detonation Transition (DDT) Run-Up Distance:**
   - Transition distance from spark ignition to sustained continuous detonation wave:
     $$L_{\text{DDT}} \le 15.0\text{ mm}$$
   - Run-up lengths exceeding $15.0\text{ mm}$ risk combustion chamber acoustic resonance destruction and throat unstart.

4. **Continuous Rotating Wave Mode Frequency & Annular Geometry:**
   - Detonation wave rotation frequency:
     $$f_{\text{det}} = \frac{n_{\text{wave}} D_{\text{CJ}}}{\pi D_{\text{annulus}}} \ge 20.0\text{ kHz}$$
   - Number of co-rotating detonation wave heads:
     $$n_{\text{wave}} \ge 2$$
   - Annular channel width gap:
     $$\Delta r_{\text{channel}} \in [2.5, 12.0]\text{ mm}$$
   - Mean annular diameter:
     $$D_{\text{annulus}} \in [50.0, 300.0]\text{ mm}$$

5. **Thermodynamic Pressure-Gain Combustion Efficiency:**
   - Net total pressure gain over classical isobaric Brayton/Humphrey cycle:
     $$\eta_{\text{gain}} = \frac{P_{\text{total, exit}} - P_{\text{total, inlet}}}{P_{\text{total, inlet}}} \ge 0.15\quad (15\%)$$

6. **Aerospike Plug Expansion Nozzle Efficiency:**
   - Altitude-compensating aerospike expansion efficiency across sea level to vacuum:
     $$\eta_{\text{nozzle}} \ge 0.92\quad (92\%)$$

7. **Specific Impulse ($I_{\text{sp}}$) & Thermal Wall Heat Flux:**
   - Vacuum specific impulse for LOX/CH4 or advanced hybrid:
     $$I_{\text{sp}} \ge 450.0\text{ s}$$
   - Maximum regenerative chamber wall heat flux limit:
     $$q_{\text{wall}} \le 35.0\text{ MW/m}^2$$
