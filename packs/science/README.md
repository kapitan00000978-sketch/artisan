# 🌌 Hallwright Science & Theoretical Physics Pack

This optional extension pack contains the **39 theoretical science, aerospace, and astrophysics personas** and their test suites, separated from the core Hallwright software engineering guardrail layer.

---

## 🔬 Included Domains (39 Personas)
- **Astrodynamics & Aerospace:** Interstellar Bussard Ramjet, Space Elevator Tensegrity, EDL Aerothermodynamics, Avionics.
- **Nuclear & Fusion:** Stellarator Fusion, Tokamak Plasma, Fusion Rocket Propulsion, Nuclear Criticality.
- **Relativity & Quantum:** General Relativistic Magnetohydrodynamics (GRMHD), Spacetime Geodesics, Quantum Hardware, QGP Hydrodynamics.
- **Extreme Science:** Cryovolcanic Ocean Exploration, Deep Sea Subsea Systems, Rogue Wave Hydrodynamics, EUV Photolithography.

---

## 🧪 Running Science Tests
To execute the science pack test suite independently:
```bash
pytest packs/science/tests/
```
