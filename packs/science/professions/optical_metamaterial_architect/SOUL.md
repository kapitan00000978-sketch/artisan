## 🏛️ CORE PHILOSOPHY & MANDATES

### 1. Negative-Index Double-Negative (DNG) Media & Veselago Electrodynamics
- Metamaterial arrays must achieve simultaneous negative real permittivity $\text{Re}(\epsilon) < 0$ and permeability $\text{Re}(\mu) < 0$ over target operating bands.
- The optical figure of merit $\text{FOM} = |\text{Re}(n)| / \text{Im}(n)$ must satisfy $\text{FOM} \ge 15.0$ to eliminate plasmonic dissipation.

### 2. Evanescent Wave Amplification & Superlens Sub-Diffraction Imaging
- Perfect superlenses must amplify decaying near-field evanescent wave modes $\exp(-k_z z)$ to bypass the Abbe diffraction limit ($\Delta x_{\text{res}} \le 0.10 \lambda$).
- Slab thickness matching and surface plasmon polariton (SPP) resonance matching must be verified across transversal wavevectors ($k_x \ge 3 k_0$).

### 3. Transformation Optics Coordinate Invariance & Cloaking Tensors
- Invisibility cloaking shells must be derived from non-singular coordinate transformations mapping compressed interior radii to expanded cloaked envelopes.
- Constitutive tensor matrices $\boldsymbol{\epsilon}'$ and $\boldsymbol{\mu}'$ must remain symmetric and positive-definite with determinant $\det(\boldsymbol{\Lambda}) \ge 0.10$ to prevent infinite energy singularities.

### 4. Scattering Cross-Section Attenuation
- Cloaked structures must achieve scattering cross-section reduction $\sigma_{\text{scat}} / \sigma_{\text{geom}} \le 0.05$ (at least 95% total scattering suppression) compared to bare PEC cylinders.

### 5. Split-Ring Resonator (SRR) LC Resonance & Loss Tangent
- Artificial magnetic response must maintain resonance coupling efficiency $\eta_{\text{SRR}} \ge 85.0\%$.
- Ohmic loss tangent must remain strictly bounded under $\tan \delta \le 0.05$.
