## 🏛️ CORE PHILOSOPHY & MANDATES

### 1. Exponential Quantum Tunneling & Barrier Calibration
- Tunneling current $I(z) = I_0 \exp(-2\kappa z)$ depends exponentially on tip-sample separation $z$, decaying an order of magnitude per Angstrom ($\kappa = \sqrt{2m\Phi}/\hbar$).
- Tunneling current setpoint $I_t$ must be maintained with sub-picoampere noise floor ($\sigma_I \le 1.0\text{ pA}$) across sub-nanometer gaps ($z \in [0.3, 1.2]\text{ nm}$).

### 2. Frequency-Modulation AFM & qPlus Sensor Dynamics
- Sub-molecular chemical bond imaging requires non-contact AFM using quartz tuning forks (qPlus sensors) with high stiffness ($k \approx 1800\text{ N/m}$) and ultra-high quality factor $Q \ge 25,000$.
- Dynamic oscillation amplitude must remain below $A \le 100.0\text{ pm}$ to probe short-range Pauli repulsive forces without chemical snap-to-contact.

### 3. Piezoelectric Scanner Hysteresis & Drift Compensation
- Open-loop piezoelectric ceramics suffer from severe hysteresis (up to 15%) and logarithmic creep.
- Active dual-axis capacitive position feedback must bound lateral drift rate to $\dot{r}_{\text{drift}} \le 5.0\text{ pm/hour}$.

### 4. Single-Atom Lateral & Vertical Manipulation
- Controlled repositioning of single adatoms (e.g., Fe, Xe, CO) on crystalline substrates (Cu(111), Pt(111)) requires precise tip height trajectories and lateral forces ($F_{\text{lat}} \in [0.5, 15.0]\text{ nN}$).
- Placement accuracy must reach $\delta r_{\text{atom}} \le 20.0\text{ pm}$ to engineer artificial quantum corrals, topological lattices, and atomic bit memories.

### 5. Ultra-High Vacuum (UHV) & Cryogenic Thermal Stability
- Ambient chamber base pressure must remain in ultra-high vacuum $P_{\text{UHV}} \le 1.0\times 10^{-10}\text{ mbar}$ to prevent ambient gas physisorption.
- Sample stage temperature must remain cryogenic $T_{\text{stage}} \le 4.5\text{ K}$ (liquid helium) to suppress thermal diffusion of adatoms.
- Tip-sample vibrational amplitude must remain under $\Delta z_{\text{vib}} \le 1.0\text{ pm}$.
