## 🏛️ CORE PHILOSOPHY & QUANTITATIVE THRESHOLDS

1. **Geometric Transit Depth ($\delta_{\text{transit}} = (R_p / R_*)^2$):**
   - Planetary radius $R_p$ over stellar radius $R_*$ squared determines baseline primary transit depth.
   - Must satisfy $\delta_{\text{transit}} \ge 1.0\times 10^{-5}$ ($10\text{ ppm}$) for detectable exoplanetary occultation.

2. **Atmospheric Scale Height ($H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{eq}}}{\mu m_u g}$):**
   - Planetary equilibrium temperature $T_{\text{eq}}$, mean molecular weight $\mu$ ($2.3\text{ amu}$ for $H_2/He$, $28\text{ amu}$ for $N_2$, $44\text{ amu}$ for $CO_2$), and surface gravity $g = G M_p / R_p^2$.
   - Scale height must satisfy $H \in [10.0, 3000.0]\text{ km}$.

3. **Transmission Spectral Feature Amplitude ($\Delta \delta_\lambda \approx 2 (R_p / R_*) (N_H H / R_*)$):**
   - Number of scale heights $N_H \in [3.0, 7.0]$ probed by optical and infrared molecular opacity.
   - Spectral feature amplitude must satisfy $\Delta \delta \ge 1.0\times 10^{-6}$ ($1\text{ ppm}$).

4. **Spectroscopic Signal-to-Noise Ratio ($\text{SNR} \ge 5.0\sigma$):**
   - Transit spectral feature detection significance must exceed the empirical $5\sigma$ Gaussian discovery benchmark to eliminate false-positive spectral claims.

5. **Stellar Activity / Spot Contamination Factor ($f_{\text{spot}} \le 0.30$):**
   - Unocculted stellar magnetic starspots alter the effective stellar spectrum (Transit Light Source effect). The unocculted spot filling factor $f_{\text{spot}}$ must not exceed $0.30$, and spot temperature differential $\Delta T_{\text{spot}} \ge 200\text{ K}$ must be accounted for.

6. **Bayesian Retrieval Goodness-of-Fit ($\chi^2_\nu \in [0.80, 1.50]$):**
   - Atmospheric forward radiative transfer modeling must converge with reduced chi-squared between $0.80$ and $1.50$ without unphysical parameter overfitting.
