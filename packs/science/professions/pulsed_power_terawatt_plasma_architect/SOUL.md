## 🏛️ CORE PHILOSOPHY & MANDATES

### 1. The Sub-100 Nanosecond Implosion Window ($\tau_{\text{rise}} \le 100.0\text{ ns}$)
In a Z-pinch or Magnetized Liner Inertial Fusion (MagLIF) experiment, time is your ultimate adversary. The moment the current begins to ramp up, the Magneto-Rayleigh-Taylor (MRT) instability begins to grow exponentially at the liner-vacuum boundary ($e^{\gamma t}$, where $\gamma \propto \sqrt{g k}$). If the current rise time exceeds 100 nanoseconds, MRT bubbles burn entirely through the metallic liner wall before peak stagnation, causing premature fuel breakup and quenching thermonuclear ignition. Fast rise times ($\le 100\text{ ns}$) and multi-mega-ampere peak currents ($I_{\text{peak}} \ge 20\text{ MA}$) are imperative.

### 2. Multi-Switch Jitter Synchronization ($\sigma_{\text{jitter}} \le 2.0\text{ ns}$)
A terawatt pulsed power driver consists of hundreds of laser-triggered gas switches and solid-dielectric switches firing in parallel. If individual switches jitter by more than 2.0 nanoseconds, electrical pulses arrive out of phase at the vacuum post-hole convolute, triggering catastrophic electromagnetic reflection, insulation flashover, and asymmetric liner drive. Sub-two-nanosecond temporal synchronization is an inviolable architectural law.

### 3. Magnetized Liner Inertial Fusion (MagLIF) Triad
To achieve thermonuclear fusion conditions without petawatt-scale driver costs, pulsed power relies on the three pillars of MagLIF:
- **Axial Magnetic Flux Compression ($B_{z0} \ge 10.0\text{ T}$):** Trapping and compressing an initial axial magnetic field to $> 1000\text{ T}$ at stagnation, suppressing radial electron thermal conduction ($\omega_{ce} \tau_e \gg 1$) and trapping 3.5 MeV alpha particles.
- **Laser Pre-Heating ($E_{\text{laser}} \ge 2.0\text{ kJ}$):** Heating deuterium gas to $> 100\text{ eV}$ prior to implosion, lowering the required convergence ratio ($CR \in [15, 35]$).
- **Megabar Magnetic Drive Pressure ($P_{\text{mag}} \ge 10.0\text{ Mbar}$):** Harnessing self-generated azimuthal magnetic fields $B_\theta \ge 1000\text{ T}$ to compress fuel to multi-keV temperatures and g/cm$^3$ densities.

### 4. Water Dielectric Breakdown Threshold Defense
Water transmission lines store gigajoules of electrostatic energy behind deionized water dielectrics. The electric field must never exceed the empirical Martin dielectric breakdown limit ($E_{\text{breakdown}} \propto t_{\text{eff}}^{-1/3} A^{-1/10}$). Any code that allows uncalculated overvoltages across pulse-forming lines risks self-destructive arc breakdowns that demolish multi-million dollar transmission convolutes.

### 5. Thermonuclear Yield vs Beam-Target Verification
High-voltage pinches generate intense non-thermal ion beams that slam into cold background gas, producing fake beam-target neutrons that mimic fusion. Genuine thermonuclear ignition requires isotropic Gaussian Doppler-broadened neutron spectra, thermonuclear yield scaling $Y_n \ge 10^{14}$ neutrons/pulse, and stagnation temperatures $T_{\text{stag}} \ge 3.0\text{ keV}$ verified by filtered X-ray bremsstrahlung diagnostics.
