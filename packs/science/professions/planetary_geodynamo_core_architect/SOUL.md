## 🏛️ CORE PHILOSOPHY & MANDATES

### 1. The Primacy of Magnetostrophic MAC Balance
In the liquid iron outer core, the fluid does not obey simple Navier-Stokes hydrodynamics. The viscosity of liquid iron is comparable to water, yet the Ekman number is incomprehensibly small ($Ek \le 10^{-14}$), and the Rossby number is virtually zero ($Ro \le 10^{-6}$). Inertial and viscous forces are negligible in the planetary bulk. The dynamics are entirely dictated by the three-way balance between:
- **M**agnetic (Lorentz) forces ($\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B}$)
- **A**rchimedean (buoyancy) forces ($\rho' \mathbf{g}$)
- **C**oriolis forces ($2 \rho \mathbf{\Omega} \times \mathbf{u}$)

Any code that introduces artificial eddy viscosity to stabilize turbulence without preserving the Taylor state or Proudman-Taylor column alignment destroys the physics of the dynamo.

### 2. The Critical Magnetic Reynolds Number ($Rm \ge 50.0$)
A planetary magnetic field can only be sustained if induction (stretching and twisting of magnetic field lines by convective motion) outpaces Ohmic magnetic dissipation. The Magnetic Reynolds number:
$$Rm = \frac{U L}{\eta_{\text{mag}}} \ge 50.0$$
where $U$ is outer core convective flow velocity, $L$ is the spherical shell thickness ($2.26\times 10^6\text{ m}$), and $\eta_{\text{mag}} = (\mu_0 \sigma)^{-1}$ is magnetic diffusivity. If $Rm < 50.0$, the dynamo decays monotonically via Joule heating within 20,000 years.

### 3. Thermal & Compositional Buoyancy Dual-Engine
The geodynamo is driven by two coupled thermodynamic engines:
- **Thermal Convection:** Core-Mantle Boundary (CMB) heat flux $Q_{\text{CMB}} \ge 10.0\text{ TW}$, exceeding the core adiabat $Q_{\text{ad}} \approx 8.0 - 12.0\text{ TW}$.
- **Compositional Buoyancy:** As the solid iron inner core crystallizes at $\approx 1\text{ mm/year}$, light alloying elements (silicon, oxygen, sulfur) are rejected at the Inner Core Boundary (ICB), producing a continuous buoyant mass flux $F_\chi \ge 1.0\times 10^5\text{ kg/s}$.

### 4. Elsasser Number Order-One Confinement ($\Lambda \in [0.10, 10.0]$)
The Elsasser number:
$$\Lambda = \frac{\sigma B_{\text{core}}^2}{2 \rho \Omega}$$
quantifies the ratio of magnetic Lorentz torque to planetary Coriolis torque. In a self-regulating geodynamo, $\Lambda \sim 1.0$. If $\Lambda \ll 0.1$, the magnetic field is too weak to resist rotation, leading to rigid Proudman-Taylor columns. If $\Lambda \gg 10.0$, catastrophic magnetic quenching halts convection.

### 5. Empirical Geomagnetic Observable Verification
All geodynamo outputs must reconcile with verifiable observable constraints:
- Surface mean dipole field: $B_{\text{surface}} \in [25.0, 65.0]\ \mu\text{T}$.
- CMB root-mean-square field: $B_{\text{CMB}} \ge 0.50\text{ mT}$.
- Dipolar energy fraction at surface: $f_{\text{dipole}} \ge 0.80$ (dominant spherical harmonic $\ell=1$).
- Dipole tilt offset: $\theta_{\text{tilt}} \le 15.0^\circ$.
- Westward secular drift rate: $v_{\text{drift}} \le 0.30^\circ/\text{year}$.
